A LETTER FROM MY HEART

To My Maa ❤️

♡ ───── ❤️ ───── ♡

My Dearest Maa, ❤️

I don't know where to begin, because there are so many things I have kept inside my heart for you.

Maybe this is not just a birthday letter. Maybe it is a collection of all the things I felt, all the memories I kept, all the prayers I made, all the tears I hid, and all the love I never knew how to explain to you.

So today, I just want to tell you everything.

I LOVE YOU, MAA. ❤️

I still remember the first time I saw you on the bus. At that time, I had no idea that the girl I was looking at would become such an important person in my life.

Then we became classmates. Then came our first proper conversation at the New Year function in 2022.

It was just a moment, but sometimes the smallest moments become the beginning of the biggest stories in our lives.

I remember March 27, when I asked you for your photo and you sent me four reels. Maybe it was a small thing for you, but for me, those little things slowly became precious memories.

And I still remember the first thing I liked about you...

Your eyes. ❤️

Slowly, without even realizing it, I started collecting memories of you.

The Mallige flowers I gave you at your office. 🌸

The ring I gave you. 💍

The time I searched for a lotus in the lake. 🪷

And the first time we met at the Ganesh temple.

Everyone else may have gone there to see Ganesh... but honestly, I was so busy looking at you that I almost forgot to look at Ganesh properly. 😂❤️

Then came August 3, 2025, at 10:10 PM.

That night, I proposed to you.

I don't know whether I expressed everything perfectly, but I know one thing — whatever I said that night came from my heart.

I also remember October 25, 2025, when your first photo became part of my story and I asked for a song.

And I remember November 14, 2025, the first time you got angry with me.

Funny thing is... even your angry face became one of the faces I love. My cute angry bird. 😂❤️

✦ ✦ ✦

You know I have always had a special belief in Lord Shiva. 🕉️

For me, Shiva represents devotion, patience, waiting and a love that doesn't simply disappear. That's one of the reasons Shiva has always been so special to me.

But after our first fight... after our first silence... something changed inside me.

I started looking at faith differently. I started believing in other Gods too. I started going to temples. Whenever I saw a God, I started praying.

I kept a Gururagavendra Swamy name/book and continued my prayers.

I did a 16 days Karyasiddi Anjaneya vrata for you. 🙏

I started making 11:11 wishes after you told me about them.

I even wrote my wishes on leaves and kept them near God's frame while praying.

And do you know what most of those wishes were about?

YOU. ❤️

Your happiness. Your well-being. Your life. Your smile.

You probably don't even know how many prayers I have silently made for you.

There is also something that happened during one nervous situation that you don't know about.

Whenever you have free time, ask me. I'll tell you everything. ❤️

✦ ✦ ✦

Maa, I have always told other people:

"Don't beg for love.
Don't beg for attention.
Don't beg for care.
Love the person who genuinely loves you.
Never lose your self-respect for someone."

I have motivated so many people with those words.

But the truth is...

That motivation never worked for me when it came to you.

I told everyone not to lose themselves for love, and then I found myself doing exactly that with you.

I lost my self-respect sometimes because I was scared of losing you.

I'm not saying that because I think it was right. I'm saying it because I want to be honest with you.

You affected me in a way nobody else ever did.

I have never been someone who cries easily because of losing people.

If someone wants to leave my life, I usually let them go.

But with you...

I cried so many times. ❤️

And I'm sorry for the times I made you cry too.

I never wanted to hurt you.

✦ ✦ ✦

I know I'm not perfect. Sometimes I tease you too much. Sometimes I become too emotional. Sometimes I become jealous. Sometimes I become possessive.

I know that isn't always easy for you.

But Maa, I don't want to control you.

I just love you so deeply that sometimes my emotions become stronger than my ability to handle them.

I love hearing about your day.

The smallest story. The smallest moment. What happened. What made you happy. What made you angry. What made you laugh.

I want you to feel comfortable sharing your little world with me.

Because talking to you is one of my happiest things. ❤️

And simply seeing you can make my whole day better.

✦ ✦ ✦

I know these days you may not love me the way I love you.

Maybe you don't feel the same way. Maybe you don't accept my love.

I don't know why. And sometimes I don't understand it.

But I don't want to blame you for your feelings. You have the right to feel whatever your heart feels.

I only want you to know mine.

I LOVE YOU EVERY DAY. ❤️

Not only when you talk to me. Not only when you are happy with me. Not only when you give me attention.

Even in silence. Even from a distance. Even when you are angry with me. Even when you ignore me.

My feelings don't simply disappear.

You are the one person I have loved this deeply.

✦ ✦ ✦

I don't know what the future has planned. I don't know whether we will get married or not. I don't know where life will take us.

But if one day we do get married, I will give my best to make you happy for as long as I breathe.

Until my last breath, I would want to see you smile.

And if life takes us in different directions and we don't get married...

I will still wish for your happiness. I will still want good things for you.

If someday someone special comes into your life, I hope you will tell me yourself.

Not because I want to interfere. Not because I want to stop you.

But because I would rather hear your truth from you than believe someone else's words about you.

I trust the person I know, not rumours about her.

✦ ✦ ✦

Maa, my biggest wish has always been very simple:

I WANT TO SEE YOU SMILE. ❤️

That's it.

Even though I secretly love seeing your angry-bird face too. 😂❤️

Your smile can change my whole mood. Talking to you makes me happy. Seeing you makes me happy.

Even a small moment with you can stay in my mind for a long time.

✦ ✦ ✦

And Maa, there is one more thing I want to say.

Before we completely leave each other's lives, or before either of us gets married — to each other or to someone else — I have one heartfelt wish.

One genuine, complete hug. 🫂

One kiss. ❤️

And one long, gentle conversation.

No anger. No ego. No misunderstandings. Just you and me talking honestly.

I want to tell you everything that I never knew how to say.

And I want to hear you too.

Not to change your decision. Not to force anything. Just because there are some conversations that the heart deserves to have at least once.

✦ ✦ ✦

Maa...

I don't know whether I will always have a place in your life.

But I know you will always have a place in mine.

You taught me things without even trying.

You made me pray more. You made me believe more. You made me cry. You made me smile. You made me jealous. You made me crazy sometimes. 😂

You made me understand how deeply a person can care about another person.

And most importantly...

You made me experience a kind of love I had never experienced before. ❤️

So if you ever wonder whether I really loved you...

YES, MAA. ❤️

I did. I do. And maybe I always will.

Not because I expect something from you. Not because I want you to owe me anything.

But simply because...

MY HEART LOVES YOU. ❤️

Thank you for every memory. Thank you for every conversation. Thank you for every smile. Thank you even for the fights. Thank you for the silence. Thank you for becoming such an important part of my story.

And if I ever hurt you...

I'm truly sorry. ❤️

If I ever made you cry...

I'm sorry. 🫂

If I ever became too much...

I'm sorry.

But never doubt one thing:

I WANTED YOUR HAPPINESS. ❤️

Maybe I didn't always know the right way to show it. Maybe I made mistakes. Maybe my love became too emotional sometimes.

But underneath all of it was one simple wish...

TO SEE YOU HAPPY. ❤️

✦ ✦ ✦

Happy Birthday, Maa. 🎂❤️

I hope you get everything you dream about. I hope you smile more than you cry. I hope you succeed in everything you choose. I hope life treats you gently.

And wherever life takes you...

Please be happy. ❤️

Because seeing you happy will always be one of my favourite things.

And if someday you look back at our story, I hope you don't remember only the fights or the silence.

I hope you remember the bus.

The New Year function. March 27. The four reels. Your eyes. The Mallige flowers. The ring. The lotus. The Ganesh temple. The angry-bird face. October 25. November 14.

And most of all...

August 3, 2025 — 10:10 PM. ❤️

The night when I finally told you what my heart had been carrying.

Whatever happens after this, those memories will always be mine.

And you will always be...

My Maa. ❤️

❤️ 🫂 ❤️

I loved you yesterday.
I love you today.
And I will carry this love with me tomorrow too.

Always. ❤️

With all my heart, Yashavantha ❤️
